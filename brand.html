Brand Guide

For An Unforgettable Experience, Just Add Water®

November 9, 2026
Prepared by DPA Branding
mike@dpabranding.com

Download Brand Standards PDF

Each logo file has its own download below. There is no combined asset pack.

Section 01

Logo

Two files: the full lockup, and the gold mark. Use them whole.

Download lockup PNG Download mark PNG

Clear space

Keep at least the height of the gold waves clear on every side. Do not crowd the mark with type.

Minimum size

Do not display the full lockup below 160 pixels wide. The mark file is 187 by 147 pixels. Do not scale it up.

Do

  • Use the lockup on paper, white, or ink.
  • Use the gold mark when the ground is navy.
  • Leave the files whole.

Do not

  • Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, outline, or add a shadow.
  • Do not place the lockup on a busy photograph without a solid field.
  • Do not redraw the waves or set the name in a different face.

Section 02

Color

Navy, water, mist, paper, ink, and the gold of the mark.

Button gold#8d7045

The mark gold is #c0995e. White type on that gold is 2.64:1, short of WCAG AA, so buttons use #8d7045, the same hue darkened until white type reaches 4.63:1. Hover is #7c633d. Navy still carries headings. Water and mist are grounds, not text on white.

Section 03

Typography

Headlines and text are Manrope. Headlines are bold. Text is regular.

Dining cruises and private events on Seattle's lakes.

Experience Seattle from a new perspective with Waterways Cruises & Events. Based in Fishermen's Terminal, we offer experiences on Lake Union and Lake Washington, from public dining cruises to fully private charters and events.

Body text is 18px on a phone and 20px on a wide screen. Buttons use Manrope.

Section 04

Buttons

Gold fill, #8d7045, with white type. Hover darkens to #7c633d. Every target is at least 48 pixels tall. On a phone, a pair of actions stacks full width.

Primary actionSecondary action

On navy

Section 05

Voice and tone

The line is “For An Unforgettable Experience, Just Add Water®”. Write in sentence case. Sound like a host on the lake: specific, calm, and hospitable.

Say dining cruise, private charter, yacht, Lake Union, Lake Washington, and Fishermen’s Terminal. Keep menus in menu language, with the dietary tags the kitchen published.

Do not claim the company is the only dining cruise in the city. Do not print star ratings. Do not invent a guest count, a season year, or a reply window.

No emoji. No exclamation stacks. No discount lead.

Section 06

Usage

  • Use the photographs already on these pages. Do not caption them as agency photography.
  • Do not add stock-named files.
  • Do not generate images. Do not enlarge a file past its pixel size.
  • The charter flyer keeps its printed title, 2027 Private Charter Flyer.
  • Agency credit on client-facing pages is DPA Branding, mike@dpabranding.com.
