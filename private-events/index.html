The fleet

The Emerald Star

Sleek and beautifully appointed, the 105 foot Emerald Star features two spacious interior salons and two open-air decks. Emerald Star’s amenities include large picture windows for panoramic views during the cruise, an overhead sound system, two granite-topped bars, and restrooms on both levels of the yacht. The Emerald Star is ideal for dining cruises, corporate and wedding events.

The Olympic Star

The Olympic Star is a spacious 90 foot yacht perfect for entertaining on the water. Guests can relax in either of the two comfortable dining salons, sip cocktails by the fully equipped deluxe bar upstairs or enjoy panoramic views of Seattle’s skyline on the outdoor deck The Olympic Star features large picture windows, flexible seating arrangements and a built-in buffet.

The Destiny

Waterways’ Destiny is the ideal venue for intimate gatherings of friends, family members and colleagues. This 64 foot yacht accommodates up to 30 guests for a buffet and up to 40 guests for a reception style event. The Destiny features an enclosed dining salon, open-air observation decks and a fully equipped bar. Guests can enjoy Seattle’s picturesque lake views while cruising in style.

The Sightseer

Our renovated historic vessel, the Sightseer, offers a one-of-a-kind experience that will have you relaxing the moment you step aboard. The enclosed lower salon features dining seating for up to 48 guests, a full bar, restrooms, and a galley. Upstairs, you’ll find an open-air covered lounge with comfortable couches and hanging chairs, creating the perfect laid-back Seattle atmosphere.

Corporate events

Events with Waterways

Waterways Cruises and Events offers perfect venues for any type of corporate event. Whether you entertain your clients with a unique dining experience on the water, reward your hard-working employees with a festive holiday party, or need a one-of-a-kind meeting space, Waterways will make the planning process for your next corporate event easy and stress-free. Contact our event managers for more information!

Waterways’ yachts can accommodate large gatherings for any type of corporate events – holiday parties, client entertainment, business meetings, team building activities and incentive events. Our yachts feature interior dining salons with wood and brass trim, spacious outdoor decks with panoramic views and areas for dancing to live music or DJ entertainment. Learn more about our yachts or contact us to schedule a personal site visit with one of our event managers.

Event Planning

Make your event planning easy with the assistance of our professional event managers! We will help you through every step of the planning process, including menu selections, entertainment and timeline. Whether extravagant or understated, we will take care of all the details so you can relax and enjoy your celebration! For easy planning of your event, we have designed Corporate Packages that offer convenient per person pricing and include yacht charter fees, catering and bar options. Packages can be easily customized to fit your unique occasion.

Waterways Catering

Catering for events aboard our yachts is provided exclusively by our experienced in-house culinary team. Our Executive Chef has designed outstanding menus that represent an array of favorite selections from appetizers to brunch buffet to plated dinner. Browse our Catering Menus and inquire about menu customization to meet your specific needs.
