Cruise Manager

Waterways is looking for a group of core leadership to join our team year-round! The On-board Cruise Manager is a mature individual, who is responsible for a successful execution of an onboard event. This position thrives on delivering the best guest experience while leading an onboard team. This individual will be dedicated and possess skills in leadership, organization, time management, excellent communication, and good judgment. Learn More Job Description Waterways is looking for a group of core leadership to join our team year-round! The On-board Cruise Manager is a mature individual, who is responsible for a successful execution of an onboard event. This position thrives on delivering the best guest experience while leading an onboard team. This individual will be dedicated and possess skills in leadership, organization, time management, excellent communication, and good judgment. Essential Duties & Responsibilities: Ability to multi-task in a fast-paced operation, while maintaining poise and hospitality. Prepare layouts based on guest count, client needs, and staffing level. Review all contract details with the crew and set expectations for the event. Manage onboard team throughout the event. Direct and help team as needed while being a gracious host to guests onboard. Assign and direct crew in their position related responsibilities, including side work, resets, and cleaning of F&B areas. Ability to reach, bend, stoop, wipe, push, pull and lift items up to 50 pounds. Direct and lead set up/tear down before and after shift. Monitor and evaluate all food, beverage, and service quality during events. Be a host with guests and ensure satisfaction is being met throughout cruise/event experience. Able to think of their feet and problem solve. Support the staff with all safety requirements, while communicating essential information regarding the event. Keep all vessels in “Show-Ready” condition. Completion of Senior Deckhand training, including, but not limited to, vessel operations, fire safety, CPR/AED, line handling, and First Aid (preferred). Be proactive in communicating with General Manager, Operations Manager, and Operations Coordinator when work order is needed or supplies are low. Responsible for accurately filling out reports (event reports, or injuries). Confirm information with the captain and have them send to appropriate parties. This position requires pre and random drug testing as mandated by the U.S. Coast Guard. Desired Background: 3 years working in the hospitality industry Minimum 1 year as a Manager/Supervisor in Food & Beverage Understands the importance of safety in the workplace and adheres to all mandated standards Proficient in Microsoft Office Work Schedule: Bulk of business occurs nights, weekends and holidays so availability during those times is a must. A flexible schedule is required. Compensation: $22/an hour (+ tips) How to Apply: To be considered for this position, please send resume and cover letter to Guest Services and Operations Director, Zach Smith, at zach@waterwayscruises.com . Perks: Waterways will assist in tracking sea time. An individual that reaches 2 yrs of service with the company and has accrued enough sea time, will be eligible for assistance from the company in obtaining their AB License, Mates License, and up to, and possibly including, 100 ton Master’s license. After three months working at Waterways, employees will receive four tickets to any standard dining cruise, to be used within a year. Waterways provides employees a Friends and Family discount of 25% off standard dining cruises. About Waterways Cruises and Events: Founded in 1994, Waterways Cruises and Events continues to provide distinctive cruising experiences on Seattle’s lakes and Puget Sound. Family owned and operated, Waterways has grown from a single yacht for private charters to include four beautiful dining and entertainment yachts, accommodating guests each year (annual figure held pending confirmation) for weddings, corporate events, special celebrations, and public dining cruises. For an Unforgettable Experience … Just Add Water! Pre-employment background checks and drug/alcohol screening required for all positions. Random drug/alcohol testing also required as mandated by the USCG.

Must be able to work with and without supervision. Must have a clean and professional appearance and work station at all times. Ability to be self-motivated and thrive under pressers. Must be punctual and reliable. Must be able to take direction from the Executive Chef and work well as part of a team. Qualification Requirements: Must have previous fine dining experience. Must have experience with Pacific Northwest cuisine. Must have high standards of sanitation and knowledge of Washington state health codes. Must possess a current Washington state food handler’s card. Able to interact with customers and other crew members in English. Positive attitude. Education Requirements: High school diploma or equivalent. Culinary degree is preferred. Essential Duties & Responsibilities: Professionally pilot and handle twin screw vessels, often in difficult marine environments. Ensure that the safety of passengers, crew, and the vessel is the highest operational priority. Adhere to all USCG rules and regulations necessary for safe vessel operations. Understand all vessel operational systems and how they function. Create a friendly, inviting environment for passengers: greet guests when they board, provide safety and other announcements; answer guest’s questions; provide an informative cruise narration; visit with guests at appropriate times; and thank guests as they disembark. Build a team environment with crew on our vessels to ensure a safe and unforgettable experience for our guest on every cruise. Handle all guest and employee issues on our vessels in a professional and courteous manner. Assist crew in the set-up or tear-down for events onboard our vessels as needed. Assist in maintaining Waterways’ vessels in excellent condition. Maintain vessel helm and engineering logs, and complete Company required pre- and post-cruise paperwork in a timely and workmanlike manner. Be the face of Waterways–always appropriately groomed, smartly dressed in a clean uniform, and courteous to passengers and other crew members. Minimum Position Requirements: USCG Merchant Mariner Certification – 100-ton License TWIC card FCC Radio Operators License CPR and First Aid credentials Basic computer skills WORK SCHEDULE: A flexible schedule is required – schedules include evenings, weekends, and holidays.
