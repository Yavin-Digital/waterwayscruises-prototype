Holiday Sights Cruise | Parade of Lights

Toast the season, sip hot cocoa, and cruise past the twinkling lights of Seattle’s Holiday Parade of Lights!

View this cruise

Join Seattle’s Annual Tradition of Holiday Parade of Lights!

Ring in the holiday cheer aboard the Sightseer! Join the celebration of the annual Holiday Parade of Lights on the lakes of Seattle with a festive, cocktail-style cruise for the whole family. Start with a champagne or sparkling cider toast, then mingle and graze at our appetizer buffet. Warm up at the hot cocoa bar, and let the kids write a letter to Santa or color a holiday masterpiece. Then step out on deck to take in the twinkling lights. Our captain will share extra narration along the way. Cruise times and routes vary depending on the date, so please check your confirmation email for boarding, departure, and return details. This is a cocktail-style event with no reserved seating or tables. Attire is at the discretion of each guest. A sweater or jacket is recommended for cooler weather when viewing from the outdoor decks. We cap this cruise at a lower passenger count due to the colder weather. Our largely enclosed, heated lower cabin offers a cozy place to warm up.

Cruise includes

3-Hour Cruise on Lake Union and Lake Washington (route and times subject to Parade of Lights route) Champagne or sparkling cider toast upon boarding Holiday appetizer buffet (see menu below) Festive Holiday Activities – hot cocoa bar, letter to Santa station, holiday-themed coloring station Breathtaking views of Seattle Captain’s light narration of sights Cocktail-style event, no reserved seating or tables All ages! Full-service bar available for the purchase of specialty cocktails, wine, beer, and non-alcoholic beverages.
