Clear space

Maintain at least 16px clear space on all sides. The dashed box is that margin. The same margin applies to every file.

Minimum size

Minimum display width is 80px. Shown here at that size. Below it, the wordmark fails. The same minimum applies to every file.

Section 02

Color Palette

Six colors. No others. Oxide red carries the poster moments. Signal red is reserved for the one thing you want clicked or called.

Oxide and Signal. Oxide red is the field, the mark, and the poster moment. Signal red is actions only: CTAs, links, and phone numbers. Never use both reds on the same element. The 5% red in the ratio is Oxide. Signal is the single action inside that share, not a second field of red.

Section 06

Usage Rules

Apply these with the logo, color, and type rules above. When a layout and a logo file disagree, the file wins.

Logo

  • Primary lockup is img/logo-site.svg, on Ink. The site header uses this file.
  • Dark lockup is img/logo-site-dark.svg, on Paper.
  • Solid lockup is img/logo-site-solid.svg, on Oxide, and only when nothing else will hold.
  • Compact lockup is img/logo.svg, with logo@2x.png and logo-default.png as rasters. On Paper.
  • Clear space is 16px on all sides. Minimum display width is 80px.

Logo — do not

  • Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, or add effects.
  • Do not place the logo on photographic backgrounds or patterns.
  • Do not swap grounds. Light artwork on Ink. Dark artwork on Paper.
  • Do not invent a wordmark-only or extra stacked set. Use the files that exist.

Color and type

  • Ink #201E1D. Oxide #AE1800. Signal #EC3013. Paper #F3F2F2. Grit 300 #D7D3D3. Grit 700 #605D5D.
  • Ratio: 70% Ink or Paper, 25% Grit, 5% red.
  • Oxide carries poster moments. Signal is the one click or call. Never both reds on the same element.
  • Archivo only. Weights 400, 800, and 900. Headlines 800. Body 400.

Copy

  • Short declaratives. No exclamation marks. No “amazing”. No discount language.
  • Price is stated plainly or not at all.
  • Paint restoration is primary. Interior is secondary. Paintless dent removal is supporting only.
  • Do not use emojis in copy, UI, or print.

Layout

  • Everything flush left. No centered text. No centered headings.
  • Zero corner radius. Cards, buttons, inputs, and images stay square.
  • Section seams are 2px solid Grit 300. Never a 1px hairline.
  • A 10px Oxide square hangs beside numbered items in place of a numeral.
  • Photography is grayscale only, cropped tight to a panel or a detail. Never a whole glossy car on a white background.

Numbered item

A 10px Oxide square replaces numerals in step lists, process flows, and ordered content.
