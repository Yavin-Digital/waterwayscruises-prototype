Silver Cloud South Lake Union

Located on the south end of scenic Lake Union, the Silver Cloud Hotel Seattle – Lake Union is two miles from downtown Seattle and five miles from the University of Washington. The hotel is located near the Fred Hutchinson Cancer Research Center, and only a 20 minute walk to the iconic Space Needle . Most of the guest rooms have spectacular waterfront views of Lake Union and Seattle. A complimentary Silver Cloud breakfast is offered daily in addition to use of our expansive fitness center. Book your Waterways Cruises customer rate For a Group Block please email lakeunion.rfp@silvercloud.com

Astra Hotel

Astra Hotel in Seattle, located in the vibrant South Lake Union area, blends innovation and luxury. With 265 guest rooms, 5,000 square feet of event space, three eateries including a rooftop lounge, and a focus on tech-forward amenities like a robot butler, Astra creates a dynamic environment for business and leisure travelers. Its mission is to foster creativity and connection while offering modern, mindful hospitality. Explore art, nature, and technology in this sleek, urban oasis.

Courtyard by Marriott Seattle Downtown/Lake Union

Steps from the Space Needle and South Lake Union, Courtyard Seattle Downtown/South Lake Union is within walking distance of Climate Pledge Arena and top attractions. Enjoy modern comforts like free high-speed Wi-Fi, flat-panel TVs, ergonomic workstations, and plush beds. Explore Pike Place Market, Chihuly Garden, MOHAI, and the Seattle Aquarium, all within two miles. Stay active in our fitness studio, or host events in our versatile meeting spaces.

EVEN Hotel Seattle Downtown - Lake Union

Stay close to the waterfront and renowned attractions at EVEN Hotel Seattle Downtown – Lake Union . Visit the Space Needle and Lumen Field just minutes away. EVEN hotel offers access to over 100 restaurants, shops, and entertainment venues, all within walking distance. The EVEN is connected to Staybridge Suites!

Hampton Inn & Suites Downtown

Get comfy in downtown Seattle at the Hampton Inn & Suites , a half-mile from the Space Needle, Museum of Pop Culture, and the Gates Foundation Discovery Center. Amazon headquarters is a mile away, and we’re a five-minute walk from the Seattle Center Monorail station, providing easy access to the Pike Place Market district. Hot breakfast, free WiFi, and our fitness center are yours to enjoy.

Hotel Moxy

In the heart of downtown Seattle, Moxy is a fun, vibrant and stylish hotel designed for “always-on guests” who are turned on by new experiences. Moxy’s lively communal spaces and energetic crew help guests have a good time — while they serve themselves when they want to. The hotel features communal areas, flexible spaces, an amped up bar experience, pink glowing lights, and tech-enabled everything.

Residence Inn by Marriott - Lake Union

The Seattle Marriott hotel is off the shores of Lake Union and blocks from downtown Seattle WA. The Residence Inn by Marriott Seattle Lake Union is located steps away from Amazon Headquarters and Seattle Cancer Care Alliance. Spacious all-suite accommodations with lake view options were designed for extended-stays featuring fully equipped kitchens and WiFi. This is right across from the Waterways Cruises Homeport! Book your Waterways Cruises customer rate

Staybridge Suites Seattle Downtown - Lake Union

The Staybridge Suites Seattle- South Lake Union offers guests a home away from home in the heart of the Emerald City of Seattle. The upscale residential style hotel is conveniently located in the South Lake Union neighborhood, steps away from beautiful Lake Union and a quick jaunt to the Downtown corridor. The all-suite hotel features full kitchens and your choice of studio or one bedroom suites. The Staybridge is connected to EVEN hotel!

Belltown Inn

The chic, understated, Belltown Inn is conveniently located in the hip Belltown neighborhood, surrounded by a great restaurant scene, near public transportation, and within easy walking distance of Pike Place Market, the Space Needle and Seattle Center grounds, Symphony Hall, Convention Center, two major malls, cruise ship terminals, the central waterfront, and so much more. Use code WATERWAYCRUISES on Belltown Inn to save 12%.

Fairmont Olympic Hotel

The iconic Fairmont Olympic Hotel . Stay at Seattle’s Grand Dame in Downtown, where timeless charm meets modern luxury. Discover the Pacific Northwest from our historic landmark hotel in the heart of Seattle. Enjoy five unique restaurants and bars, a full-service luxury spa, an indoor pool, solarium, and health club. Everything you need is here and within walking distance of the city’s main attractions, ready to create your ideal experience.

Hotel FIVE

Stay smack dab in the middle of everything. Hotel FIVE ’s fun and funky décor fits right in with the vibrant city surrounding it. This award-winning hotel is mere blocks from Pike Place Market and has easy access to the iconic Space Needle, the glistening Seattle Waterfront, and world-class shopping and dining. There’s no better jumping-off point for exploration. Book your Waterways Cruises customer rate

Hyatt Olive 8

Stay at Hyatt Olive 8 , our boutique hotel near downtown Seattle offers a walkable destination to iconic spots like South Lake Union and Capitol Hill neighborhoods, or catch a game at Husky Stadium. After a day exploring, sophisticated rooms or a spacious Presidential Suite await. Our wellness hotel offers ample opportunities to decompress, including an indoor pool and a fitness center with Peloton bikes and yoga studio to prioritize your health.

Hyatt Regency Seattle

Located in the heart of the Emerald City, Hyatt Regency Seattle is the city’s first LEED Gold-Certified hotel. Our downtown hotel is just a short walk from both the Seattle Convention Center and the Link Light Rail, and close to Pike Place Market, Lumen Field, Pacific Place and Lake Union Park. Whether you’re here for work or play, our spacious rooms and suites and in-house restaurants and bars make our hotel the ideal place to stay.

The Maxwell Hotel

Step outside and enjoy the masterpiece that is the Emerald City. The Maxwell Hotel is ideally situated in Uptown Seattle, at the base of Queen Anne Hill, adjacent to Seattle Center, home to Climate Pledge Arena and the iconic Space Needle. Book your Waterways customer rate

Palihotel Seattle

Palihotel Seattle , located in the heart of downtown, near Pike Place Market, blends eclectic design with Pacific Northwest charm. The boutique hotel offers 96 guest rooms, a cozy lobby, a bar, and a coffee outpost. It also houses The Hart and The Hunter, a Southern-inspired restaurant serving local flavors. The property, set in a historic building, provides a stylish and approachable stay for travelers and locals alike. Book your Waterways Cruise customer rate

Residence Inn by Marriott - Downtown Convention Center

Treat your family to a memorable stay at one of the top downtown Seattle hotels— Marriott Residence Inn Seattle Downtown Convention Center. Maximize your visit in apartment-style suites with fully equipped kitchens. Pets are welcome, and we offer family-friendly amenities including free WiFi. After a day of work or adventure in downtown Seattle, unwind at our lobby bar. Fuel up for a busy day at the convention center with complimentary hot breakfast and premium coffee.

Thompson Seattle

With a sleek glass façade offering dramatic views of Puget Sound, Thompson Seattle embodies the beauty of the Emerald City, just steps from Pike Place Market. At the connection of downtown and Belltown, explore the culture of Seattle from our hotel while soaking in its sights, sounds, and flavors from a distinctly local perspective. Our boutique hotel’s 152 rooms and suites ensure you live like a local in modern style, while our lobby-level restaurant and rooftop cocktail lounge—The Nest—bring the best of downtown Seattle and the waterfront within sight.

Embassy Suites Bellevue

The Embassy Suites Bellevue is your base along the I-90 Business Corridor. We’re near the junction of I-405 and I-90 in Bellevue, less than five miles from downtown. Amazon, Microsoft, T-Mobile, and other top companies are within 10 minutes. Downtown Seattle, Lumen Field and T-Mobile Park are 10 miles away and Sea-Tac Airport SEA is 12 miles. Enjoy our complimentary breakfast and free evening reception with snacks and drinks.
