# Official Waterways video loops

Channel: Waterways Cruises and Events, https://www.youtube.com/@waterwayscruises

These 15 loops were cut from that channel only. Each is silent, 8 seconds, 1280×720, with an H.264 MP4, a WebM, a mobile MP4, and a JPG poster in `assets/video/`. Small screens use the mobile file through `<source media="(max-width: 639px)">`. Offscreen videos wait until they are near the viewport. `prefers-reduced-motion` shows the poster only.
