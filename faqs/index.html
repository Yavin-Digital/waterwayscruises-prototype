Policies

Does Waterways operate during the winter months? What if there is inclement weather?

Yes. Waterways Cruises operates year-round, rain or shine! If inclement weather should make it u nsafe for the passengers, crew , or captain , the captain may choose to alter the cruise route, return to port during a cruise, or remain dockside for the length of the event.

What happens if a cruise is canceled by Waterways Cruises?

Cruises are rarely cancelled. Customers will be notified at least 12 hours prior to a cancellation, except in the case of unexpected, severe weather which results in cancellation of a cruise while a vessel is underway. If a cruise is canceled, customers will be allowed to reschedule to another cruise free of charge. If you are booked for a private charter, your contact will reach out to you with this information.

What is Waterways’ smoking policy?

All our vessels and dock are non-smoking and non-vaping, including all outer decks, in line with Washington State law and U.S. Coast Guard regulations.

Do you have a Lost & Found?

We have a Lost & Found bin at our office – please email us at contact@waterwayscruises.com to best coordinate a pick up date and time. Our policy is to discard Lost & Found materials after 30 days.

Can I bring my pet with me?

Only animals clearly meeting the definition of a service animal under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) are permitted onboard our vessels. All other animals will be denied passage. If you have any questions, please reach out to us at least 3 business days before the date of your cruise. The ADA defines a service animal as any dog individually trained to work or perform tasks for an individual with a disability, as defined by the ADA. The work done or tasks performed by the service animal must be directly related to the handler’s disability. A “comfort”, “emotional support” or “therapy animal” does NOT meet the definition of a service animal. If the animal is not individually trained to do work or perform a task in support of a person with a disability, it is considered a “pet” under the ADA. All “pets” will be denied passage on Waterways Cruises.

Cancellations and Pricing

What does the cost of my ticket include?

Individual ticket prices include a Port Charge, Fuel Surcharge, Service Charge, and Washington State sales tax. Any additional enhancements should be purchased separately. These charges are paid at the time your reservation is booked. Gratuity is defaulted at 15% at the time of your reservation. However, guests are welcomed to adjust this amount, tip on board with cash, or tip through other on-board purchases . Gratuity is always appreciated, but not expected. Prices and schedules are subject to change.

Does Waterways Cruises offer senior or military rates?

Waterways Cruises offers reduced rates for seniors over 65 years of age and members of the military on most of our public cruises with the exception of some specialty cruises. Please check each cruise description for specific information in this regard. If you are a member of the military, please present your military ID upon boarding your cruise.

Is a gratuity included in the price of my cruise tickets?

Your ticket price does NOT include a Gratuity. Gratuity is defaulted to 15% at the time of your reservation. However, guests are welcomed to adjust this amount at their discretion. For guests in a party of 10+, an auto-gratuity of 20% is applied. Gratuity is always appreciated, but not expected. If you have enjoyed the cruise experience and purchased additional items onboard, gratuities for our hardworking staff are always greatly appreciated! A Gratuity in the range of 15% to 25% of the cost of your tickets and any onboard purchases is appropriate. Gratuities may be paid in cash, added to your credit card payment for onboard purchases, or paid at the time of making your reservation. Some customers prefer to pay their ticket gratuity in cash while on board.

Do you accept cash?

Waterways Cruises offers a cashless cruising experience. Only Visa, MasterCard, Discover, and debit cards can be used for onboard purchases. Some customers like to bring cash to tip, however, cash CANNOT be used for onboard purchases. The hope is to simplify the experience for our guests to avoid the need to carry cash and to reduce transaction time at the bar and at the end of each cruise.

If I cancel my tickets, can I get a refund?

All purchases are final. All payments are non-refundable. The only possible way to receive a refund is through purchase of Trip Protection. Trip Protection is purchasable during booking – this ensures full refunds in the event that you need to cancel your cruise. Should you need to cancel, Waterways must be contacted at least 2 hours prior to the event time. Please contact us via phone or email and leave a message if we are not in office. Without Trip Protection, cruise reservations may be rescheduled by the purchaser with advance notice of at least five business days prior to the cruise date. If purchaser must reschedule within the five-day period prior to the cruise date, a reservation change fee of $25 per person will be incurred. Should Waterways cancel a cruise for any reason, the purchaser will be contacted by phone/email and can rebook the cruise on another date. In this event, a reservation change fee is not required to be paid at the time of rebooking.

What is Trip Protection?

Trip Protection protects your financial investment of the tickets, including taxes and fees – should you not be able to attend for any reason. Purchasing Trip Protection is the only way to receive a refund on a Waterways Cruise. Trip Protection must be exercised at least 2 hours prior to your departure. Please contact us via phone or email and leave a message if we are not in office. Cruise reservations may be rescheduled by the purchaser with advance notice of at least five days prior to the cruise date, without a reservation change required. If the purchaser must reschedule within the five-day period prior to the cruise date, a reservation change fee of $25 per person will be incurred.

How do I purchase tickets for Waterways Cruises?

You can purchase tickets online or by phone. Waterways’ office hours are limited as we are a small team, but someone will be available to respond to emails Monday – Friday 9-4 p.m. . Our email address is: contact@waterwayscruises.com or you may leave a voicemail by calling our office at 206-223-2060. Payment is required in full at the time of the reservation. Tickets may be purchased using Visa, MasterCard, Discover credit, debit cards or a Waterways Cruises gift card. Tickets must be purchased in advance and are required for all public dining cruises. Same day tickets are subject to availability. Tickets may not be purchased on premises if the cruise is full. Please ask an available crew member for assistance .

Will I receive a confirmation of my purchased tickets?

You will receive a confirmation emailed to the email address provided at the time of purchase. The confirmation will provide all cruise information including a map, the Departure Location, the Boarding Time, and Departure Time. Please be prepared to present your email confirmation upon boarding or the name under the reservation. If you need to change booking information, please contact us at least 48 hours prior to the departure of your cruise. Be sure and check your confirmation for the Departure Location and Boarding Time to assure that you arrive at the correct departure port on time for your cruise!

If I miss my cruise, can I get a refund?

There are no refunds or rescheduling options if you miss your cruise. Please arrive early for boarding and plan accordingly.

General

What is the dress code for Waterways Cruises?

Guests aboard our dinner and special event cruises are encouraged to dress in business casual to formal attire. We recommend collared shirts, dress pants, blouses, skirts and dresses. While jackets and ties are welcome, they are not required. Please note that casual attire such as shorts, tank tops, flip-flops, and athletic wear is not permitted. As it may be breezy on the open-air decks, we recommend that you bring a light jacket or sweater on all cruises in the event you wish to enjoy the sights from the open-air decks.

How steady is the cruising? Will I get seasick?

Waterways yachts are stable, gentle vessels. It is very rare for a guest to become sick, particularly since we cruise on the calm, interior waters of Lake Washington and Lake Union. If you are sensitive to motion sickness, we recommend that you take advantage of preventative measures that are available to you.

Are the boats ADA accessible?

Our boats are accessible to those with limited mobility but are not ADA compliant due to the unique nature of the marine environment. There are raised thresholds throughout the dock and the vessel . These can require guests to move down a ramp , walk up a wooden step, aboard the vessel over a gap, and a final 3 inch raised threshold to board the vessel. When boarding at Lake Union Piers (901 Fairview Avenue N), the ramp from the pier to the floating dock is fairly steep and has an abrupt edge. The width is 33.5″ between the handrails. Click here to view a video of our homeport dock and accessibility. Staff will be on hand to h elp guests with limited mobility. We can accommodate guests in wheelchairs if guests are able to take assisted steps through our raised thresholds onto the vessel. Wheelchairs must be portable and collapsible . However, the upper decks of our vessels are not accessible for wheelchairs. Restrooms are located on the lower level on all vessels but are not certified ADA accessible. There is a decent sized walk and staircase from the parking lot to the dock. Please keep this distance in mind if you have any mobility issues. For cruises out of Fishermen’s Terminal on our Sightseer vessel, we are able to provide a ramp for the boat with at least one business day’s notice. Please call 206-223-2060 during regular business hours if you have any questions. Because of their unique design and regulatory characteristics, excursion vessels like our boats – not including cruise lines – are not subject to the ADA Accessibility Guidelines for Buildings and Facilities (ADAAG). With that said, we make every reasonable effort to assist and accommodate passengers with special needs and sincerely apologize for not being ADA compliant. We are grateful that the newest boat in our fleet (Sightseer) is more accessible than our yachts and would love to retrofit all our boats in the future.

What is the Senior/Military Discount?

The discount applies to anyone over the age of 65, any veteran, or active member of the military. The Senior/Military discount is not applicable for all Waterways Cruises. Check for it’s availability under your ticket information at checkout.

Can I ride on any of your vessels if I use a wheelchair or a walker?

Our boats are accessible to those with limited mobility but are not ADA compliant due to the unique nature of the marine environment. There are raised thresholds throughout the vessel that require guests to take a step or two. Click here to view a video of our homeport dock and accessibility. We are able to provide a ramp on site only for our Sightseer vessel with at least one business day’s notice. When boarding at Fisherman’s Terminal Dock 9 (3919 18 th Ave W), there is a ramp from the shore to a floating dock and a ramp from the float to the boat that are smoothly accessible by passengers in wheelchairs or using walkers. The width is 42″ between the handrails. When boarding at Lake Union Piers (901 Fairview Avenue N), the ramp from the pier to the floating dock is fairly steep and has an abrupt edge. The width is 33.5″ between the handrails. Our crew are available to assist passengers as needed. Please note that using the boarding ramps involves a tight 90-degree corner and that slopes can exceed 1:12 depending on lake conditions. If you have questions about accessibility at other destinations, please call 206-223-2060 during regular business hours.

Are children allowed on Waterways’ cruises?

Children are welcome aboard our family-friendly cruises, such as our Weekend Brunch, Sunday Supper, and select Special Events cruises. There is no charge for Children 2 and under, however all passengers must be accounted for. Please select ticket option Child (2 and under) when making your reservations. Also, please request a highchair if needed when making a reservation. Dinner Cruises are designed for a more elegant experience and are restricted to guests who are 13 and older. Cocktail and party cruises are restricted to guests who are 21 and over.

Do I get a private table, and how is seating assigned?

All standard public dining cruise tickets, Dinner, Brunch, and Supper, include a private table. There is pre-assigned table seating on all our dining cruises and some Special Event Cruises. You will be seated at your own private table, with location depending upon the size of your party. Some Special Event Cruises offer limited cocktail-style seating rather than private tables. We assign seats based on customer needs, requests, and the capacity for each cruise.

What is a guaranteed window table?

Each dining cruise has window seating and non-window seating. Window tables are first come first serve – purchasing a guaranteed window table ensures your party will receive one. Depending on cruise bookings and party sizes, some cruises will offer more window tables than others. You can guarantee placement of your party at a window table at the time you complete your reservation online by paying for a guaranteed window table upgrade.

Can I request to be seated with another party onboard the cruise?

Yes. If you have multiple guests of your party booking separately, and would like to be seated together, please include a common party name in the “ Party Name to be seated with ” section or in the booking notes while making your reservation. We will be sure to seat your guests together at your Private Table.

Do all Waterways vessels operate under supervision of the U.S. Coast Guard?

Yes. Our vessels are regulated and certified by the U.S. Coast Guard. All crew are licensed and trained, as required by the USCG, to ensure everyone’s safety. Life preservers and all lifesaving equipment, including child-size life vests are onboard as required. The U.S. Coast Guard requires that all passengers over age 18 have valid photo identification.

Do you offer sailgating cruises?

We currently do not offer public sailgating cruises – all sailgating cruises are full vessel buyouts. Please fill out a Request for Proposal to get more information regarding sailgating on one of our private charters.

Departure, Directions, Schedules

What are the departure locations?

Waterways public dining cruises primarily depart from Dock 9 at Fishermen’s Terminal in Seattle. 3919 18th Ave W, Seattle, WA 98119 Some public cruises, such as our Seattle Lakes Taco Bar and Sip & Sunset , will depart from the Museum of History and Industry (MOHAI). 860 Terry Ave N, Seattle, WA 98109 – Slip 6 Some special events may have a different departing location. Be sure and check your confirmation and booking information for the Departure Location and Boarding Time! DIRECTIONS & PORTS

What is the difference between boarding time and departure time?

Boarding Time is the earliest you and your guests may board the vessel. It is typically 30 minutes before Departure Time. You will not be allowed to board before this time, unless confirmed with a sales representative. Departure Time is the time of the cruise’s departure from the Departure Location. Passengers must board the vessel by departure time as the yacht will leave promptly at Departure Time. The yacht will not wait for passengers who have not boarded by Departure Time. There are no refunds for missing your cruise. You are free to board your cruise any time after the scheduled Boarding Time until Departure Time. We recommend allowing plenty of time for parking and getting to the vessel. Please plan accordingly. Your cruise leaves promptly at the listed Departure Time and passengers will not be able to board once the vessel is underway.

Food and Beverage

What kind of drinks are available aboard Waterways Cruises?

A wide range of handcrafted cocktails, wine, beer, and non-alcoholic beverages are available for purchase during your cruise from our full-service bars. Please bring a valid ID if you would like to purchase alcoholic beverages onboard. We accept Visa, MasterCard, Discover, and debit. Gift cards and cash cannot be used toward the purchase of drinks on board.

Is food included?

All Standard Cruises (Dinner, Brunch, and Supper) as well as some Specialty Cruises, include food. Menus can be found in your cruise description on the Waterways Website. Depending on cruise type, food can range from small bites to full coursed dinners. You can find this information in your cruise description on our website.

Can I change my selected entrée for the dinner cruise?

You can change your dinner entree up to 2 business days prior to your cruise departure as we must communicate these changes to our kitchen staff. Please call our office at 206.223.2060 during normal business hours or email contact@waterwayscruises.com for assistance.

What if I have food allergies?

Please note any dietary restrictions or food allergies at the time of booking in the ‘ Anything else you’d like us to know?’ section. While we do our best to accommodate requests, meals are prepared in shared, compact maritime kitchens, and we cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment or prevent cross-contamination . Requests not noted in advance may not be able to be accommodated due to limited onboard kitchen space.

Do you offer vegetarian or vegan options?

Yes. Vegetarian and vegan options are described on each menu. You can add a note to your reservation in the “Anything else you’d like us to know?” tab. Please check menus on cruise description pages before booking. If you have questions , you may call 206.223.2060 during normal business hours or email contact@waterwayscruises.com for assistance .

Gift Cards, Promo Codes, and Discounts

How do I purchase or redeem a gift card?

Waterways’ gift cards may be purchased online. Gift cards can be applied toward the purchase of tickets for any public Waterways cruise. You can redeem your gift card online or by phone. Our email address is contact@waterwayscruises.com or you may leave a voicemail by calling our office at 206-223-2060. We ask that you please include your gift card code in your email. Gift cards are only valid for purchase of tickets and cannot be used for on-board purchases.

Can I use my Waterways Gift Card on board?

Gift cards are NOT valid for on board service. Gift Cards may only be used toward purchase of tickets. You can redeem them online or by phone when you go to book your cruise. Please include the gift card code in your email.

How do I redeem a Groupon?

If you have purchased a Groupon this year, please check your confirmation email from Groupon and follow the link within the PDF to book your cruise with your code. If you encounter any issues, please contact us at 206.223.2060 or at contact@waterwayscruises.com. Groupons purchased prior to 2024 should be emailed to Waterways Cruises at contact@waterwayscruises.com with a screenshot of your Groupon and any additional information (what cruise you were looking to book, etc.) so we are able to locate the information as quickly as possible. Groupons cannot be combined with any other promo codes or discounts

Can I combine promo codes with other discounts, codes, or Groupons?

No, promo codes cannot be combined with any other discounts, promo codes, or Groupons. Each promo code is intended to be used independently, ensuring you receive the best possible deal without overlap. Each promo code we offer has its own specific parameters and expiration date. Be sure to check the terms associated with the code, including its validity period and any restrictions on what it can be applied to, so you can enjoy the discount as intended.

Special Occasions

Am I allowed to decorate on public cruises?

No confetti, glitter, balloons, or open flames are allowed on board. Guests are welcome to bring small celebrational decor on public cruises. Please make a booking note when making your reservation so our crew can be notified accordingly. All decor needs to be table top unless for a private or semi private cruise. Please contact your sales representative with any questions regarding decor allowed on board for private or semi private cruising.

Can I bring a birthday cake on board or can I order a cake for my special occasion?

Cakes that do not require refrigeration are allowed to be brought onboard. Your server can provide dessert plates, forks, and a cake spatula for your party to distribute the cake. They can also cut and serve the cake when you are ready and a cake cutting fee of $2.00 per person will be charged. The cake cutting fee for wedding or tiered cakes is $3.00 per person. Please call 206-223-2060 at least 72 hours prior to your cruise to make arrangements for this service. For cake purchases, we are happy to offer cake for your celebration! Cakes are available for purchase during ticket booking and serve up to 6 people. We offer chocolate cake with chocolate frosting or white cake with vanilla frosting. Orders must be placed at least 72 hours prior to your cruise departure time. Some cruises do not offer purchase of cakes and party packages.

Private Events

How do I request more information about private events?

We are excited that you are interested in receiving more information about a private event on one of our vessels! In addition to full private charters, we also offer semi – private events such as upper deck rentals and public buyouts. To learn more about our event rates, menus and vessel availability, please fill out our Request a Proposal form. We will work to respond to your request within 24-48 hours. Please note that this time may be extended during peak seasons (summer season and holidays). The cost of a private even is dependent on many factors, and therefore we will be able to to get the most accurate information in the quickest way possible through a Request a Proposal (RFP) form. We kindly ask you complete the form before asking for prior to requesting a quote or contacting our office directly.

What are the departure locations for private events?

Private events leave from the port named for that charter. Public dining cruises primarily leave from Fishermen's Terminal Dock 9, 3919 18th Ave W, Seattle, WA 98119. Sightseer departs the Museum of History and Industry (MOHAI), 860 Terry Ave N, Seattle, WA 98109, Slip 6. Other ports are listed on the directions pages.

Can I use a gift card on a private event?

No, gift cards are only available to use for public cruises.

Is a gratuity included in the cost of my private event?

No, your charter costs do NOT include a Gratuity. Gratuity of 20% or higher is recommended. You can discuss gratuity with your sales representative as it cannot be paid onboard the vessel.

Can I bring a birthday cake onboard or can I order a cake for my special occasion?

Cakes that do not require refrigeration are allowed to be brought onboard. Our staff will cut and serve the cake when you are ready. An additional cake cutting fee will be charged per guest based on the type and size of cake. (A cake cutting fee of $2.00 per person will be charged. The cake cutting fee for wedding or tiered cakes is $3.00 per person.) Please contact your sales representative to discuss details of your specific event.

What decor can I bring for my private event?

You are welcome to bring the following select décor onboard: Centerpieces Floral Complimenting décor Swag bags Command strips* *With Captain’s approval prior to placement/use throughout the vessel Clients are prohibited from bringing the following items: Confetti or glitter Silly string Fog machines Open flame candles or fire Push pins, tacks, tape, glue, stickers Anything adhered to the wood varnish If you have any questions about specific items you would like to bring onboard, please contact your sales representative.

Do you have additional questions?

Please send us an email at events@waterwayscruises.com or call us at 206-223-2060 and leave a voicemail for answers to any additional questions. Waterways’ office hours are as we are a small team, but someone will be available to respond to emails and voicemail messages Monday – Friday from 9:30 am – 4:00 pm.

Office

What are Waterways Cruises' office hours?

Waterways’ office hours are limited during this time, but someone will be available to respond to emails Monday – Friday. The best way to reach us outside of office hours is via our email address at contact@waterwayscruises.com or you may leave a voice mail by calling our office at 206-223-2060.
